# Harmonic Ear Training

A minimalist, editorial ear-training web application designed for classical pianists and music students to develop **functional hearing** (gravitational tension and resolution) in major keys.

## Pedagogical Highlights

1. **Two-Stage Harmonic Testing:**
   * **Stage 1 (Core):** Identify functional archetypes: **Tonic (T)**, **Subdominant (S)**, or **Dominant (D)**.
   * **Stage 2 (Optional):** Identify exact Roman numerals (**I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°**).
2. **Keyboard Close-Position Voicings:**
   * Grounded baritone bass line ($F_2\text{--}G_3$, centered at $C_3$) providing unambiguous root fundamentals.
   * Right-hand 3-voice close triads ($\text{span} \le 9\text{--}12$ semitones around Middle C) fusing into unified harmonic colors rather than distracting melodic leaps.
3. **Smooth Voice Leading:**
   * Parsimonious voice-leading engine ($L_2$ displacement minimization) preserving common tones and moving stepwise to nearest inversions.
4. **Editorial Aesthetic:**
   * Henle Urtext ivory paper and ebony dark themes with classical typography and zero arcade distraction.
5. **Zero Dependencies & Zero Setup:**
   * 100% vanilla HTML, CSS, and Tone.js synthesis via CDN. No npm, no build steps, runs directly in any modern browser.
