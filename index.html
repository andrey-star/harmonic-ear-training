Notes on Functional Harmony & Voice Leading

In functional tonal harmony (Hugo Riemann's system), every diatonic chord belongs to one of three primary functional archetypes:

Tonic Family (T)

Chords: I, iii, vi

Rest, home, resolution. The submediant (vi) and mediant (iii) share two pitch classes with I and act as soft tonic substitutes.

Subdominant Family (S)

Chords: ii, IV

Departure, pre-dominant forward motion. Both contain scale degree 4, naturally pulling toward the dominant.

Dominant Family (D)

Chords: V, vii°

Tension and tonal drive. Both contain the leading tone (scale degree 7) and scale degree 2, urgently demanding resolution to the tonic.

Smooth Voice Leading (Nearest Inversions)

Common tones are retained in the same register while moving voices step by minimal intervals (semitones or whole tones). A grounded bass line in the baritone register (F2 to G3) defines the functional root without sub-bass distraction.
